# Sharing your opportunity on Instagram — a guide for Meanwhile Gallery

Patronage can turn any of your listings into a ready-to-post Instagram image — a
Story or a feed Post — built automatically from your opportunity's details. No
design software, no templates to fill in. This guide walks you through it.

---

## 1. Open your listing

Go to your opportunity's page on Patronage (e.g. your open call or commission).
At the top of the page, next to **Save**, you'll see a **Share** button. Tap it.

A panel opens with a live preview of your share card on the left and the controls
on the right. Everything you change updates the preview instantly.

---

## 2. Choose a format

At the top of the controls:

- **Story 9:16** — full-screen vertical, for Instagram/Facebook Stories.
- **Post 4:5** — the tall portrait size for your main feed grid.

You can save both from the same panel — make a Story *and* a Post for the same
listing.

---

## 3. Choose a layout

Patronage offers three layouts and picks the best one for your listing
automatically — but you can switch between them:

- **Text** — a clean, typographic card. Best when you don't have a strong image.
- **Image** — leads with your poster or hero image. Best when you have a striking
  graphic (see image tips below).
- **Feature** — puts the **fee / funding amount** front and centre in a bold
  block. Great for commissions or grants with a headline figure.

If a layout is greyed out, it isn't available for that listing (for example,
**Image** needs an uploaded image, and **Feature** needs a dollar figure).

---

## 4. Choose a theme

Pick **Dark** or **Light** to match the look of your feed. Both use the
Patronage palette (black / white / a single red accent). Your discipline tags,
deadline, location and the @patronage mark all adjust automatically.

---

## 5. Frame your image (Image layout only)

Because posters and photos come in all shapes and focal points, the **Image**
layout lets you set exactly how yours sits in the frame:

- **Drag** the image in the preview to reposition it.
- Use the **zoom slider** to scale it up and crop in on the part that matters.
- Hit **Reset** to return to the default framing.

The card always stays fully covered — you can't drag it past the edges.

---

## 6. Save and post

- **Save for Instagram Story / Save image** downloads the finished PNG to your
  device (it also copies the listing link to your clipboard).
- **Copy link** grabs the direct URL to your listing on Patronage.

Then, in Instagram:

1. Create a new Story or Post and select the saved image.
2. Paste the copied link — add it as a **Story link sticker**, or put it in your
   caption / link-in-bio so artists can tap straight through to apply.

---

## Tips for getting the most out of it

- **Upload a good hero image to your listing first.** A high-resolution poster or
  exhibition graphic makes the **Image** layout sing. Low-resolution logos are
  better left to the **Text** or **Feature** layouts.
- **Lead with the number** for funded opportunities — the **Feature** layout makes
  the fee impossible to miss as someone scrolls.
- **Post a Story and a Post.** The Story drives quick taps via the link sticker;
  the Post stays on your grid as a permanent reference.
- **Tag @patronage** in your caption — the card already carries the mark, and it
  helps us reshare your call to our wider audience.
- **Re-share as the deadline nears.** Generate a fresh card a week out — the
  deadline shown is always pulled live from your listing.
